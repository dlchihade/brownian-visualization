How to interpret this visualization:

  • Each path represents a patient from the dataset
  • Starting positions are based on three neural metrics: alpha_NET_mean (x), norm_SN_avg (y), and norm_LC_avg (z)
  • Colors represent patient's animal preference (most frequently mentioned animal type)
  • Brownian motion shows potential variation/uncertainty in measurements
  • Patients who mentioned predators have constrained movement in the x-axis
  • Patients who mentioned farm animals have constrained movement in the y-axis
